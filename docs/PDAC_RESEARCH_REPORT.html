The pipeline prioritises PDAC driver transcription factors, selects and trains synthetic parts, assembles Boolean+ODE circuits, optimises sequence, designs CRISPR guides, and scores everything on a Pareto front. The new idea, in one sentence

Regulatory Attractor Control (RAC) fits a bistable graph dynamical system so that real PDAC cell states are its stable attractors, then designs synthetic circuits as the minimal repression that moves the disease attractor toward the healthy basin — with the model's "which genes are load-bearing" predictions checked against CRISPR screens it never saw during fitting.

04

RAC — the new method Module VIII

Why a plain graph-neural-network scorer wasn't enough — and what replaces it.

A GNN that scores circuits learns a correlation, not a mechanism. RAC instead learns the dynamics of the regulatory network and treats a synthetic circuit as a control input to those dynamics. The regulatory network is a bistable map

x ← σ( gain · ( W·x + b ) )

whose viable high-activation fixed point is fit to the 54 DepMap PDAC cell states, while a dead low-activation fixed point is held stable. Loss of viability is then literally collapse to the dead attractor. W is masked to the co-expression graph, sign-anchored, and annotated with 2,120 JASPAR promoter-motif edges for directionality.

What makes it new

The dynamics are asked to agree with three independent real evidence streams that no prior method combines this way: (1) bulk/cell-line steady states as attractors, (2) sequence→chromatin causality via Enformer / the from-scratch long-range model as a counterfactual oracle, and (3) CRISPR loss-of-function as the ground truth for which nodes are load-bearing. Circuit design becomes optimal control over that landscape.

Graph

386 TF nodes · 7,492 co-expression edges · 3,103 motif-supported · CNA on 385 nodes (80 amplified) · fixed-point error 0.030 (GPU).

Control design

minimal essential-safe repression set moving the attractor toward the GTEx-healthy direction; the disease attractor shifts measurably toward the dead basin, sparing essential activity.

05

Validation — out of modality, honestly reported

DepMap CRISPR is never used to fit the dynamics. Whether attractor-collapse predicts CRISPR essentiality is therefore a genuine cross-modality test — reported with bootstrap CIs and centrality baselines, not a single flattering number.

Definitive campaign — grid + 40-fit ensemble + 50k permutation

A heavy sweep (nodes 400/600/800 × threshold 0.30/0.35/0.40) shows sparser, focused graphs win — best is 400 nodes / thr 0.4; adding lower-variance TFs lowers AUC. At best config: point AUC 0.653 [0.539, 0.759], 50,000-permutation p = 0.0022. The 40-member bootstrap ensemble mean is 0.606 [0.475, 0.650] — significant at the point estimate, modest and noisy under heavy resampling. Reported both ways, on purpose.

The strong result — leave-cell-line-out generalization

Refit the dynamics on 53 cell lines, then test the held-out one: the held-out real cell state is a 2× lower-residual fixed point than a permuted null (0.064 vs 0.128, Wilcoxon p ≈ 0). The learned regulatory dynamics generalize to PDAC cell lines they were never fit on — a cleaner, stronger validation than the essentiality AUC, and evidence the attractor structure is real rather than memorized.

Single-cell test — an honest negative that corrected us

Real TISCH2 PDAC single-cell (57,443 cells; 11,401 annotated malignant, 24 patients) gave an in-vivo tumour-intrinsic graph. It is worse than the cultured cell-line graph at predicting CRISPR essentiality (AUC 0.51–0.55 vs 0.65) — sensible, since DepMap CRISPR is measured in cell lines. Its apparent selective-dependency signal (0.63–0.66) is noise: only 3 positive nodes, collapsing to 0.43 at another threshold. A power audit then settled it — of 1,164 TFs with CRISPR data, only 7 are PDAC-selective. The earlier "composition confound caps the selective signal" hypothesis is refuted; it is a biological power ceiling.

What did not work — reported anyway

Collapse shows no signal for PDAC-selective dependency (AUC ≈ 0.49) — bulk co-expression captures transcriptional modules, not lineage-selective essentiality. And the model's regulatory coupling does not predict DepMap co-essentiality (tested on 39,340 gene-pairs, ρ ≈ −0.01, null). Both negatives are shipped in the artifacts.

Stage 2 — the disease-residual objective is live

The curriculum stage that learns PDAC-minus-normal (progression_state_residual) now runs on genuinely paired Panc1 ↔ mark-matched healthy shards, initialised from the trained healthy prior. residual_delta: 0.000000 (unpaired) → +0.445470 (paired) — ACTIVE. This is the point of the whole programme: the residual was previously unmeasurable (healthy-only corpus), then silently inert.

Two real defects found, recorded not papered over. (1) Unpaired data makes Stage 2 a no-op — residual_delta reads 0 at every step while the profile loss still falls, so a run can look successful while learning no residual. (2) chromatin-compile and chromatin-pair disagree on chromosome order — compile emits lexicographic (chr19 before chr2), pairing sorts numerically, so the stream runs backwards and aborts: the paired-state path could not run on ENCODE-compiled human data at all. Fixed with a resort step that changes only row order.

Honest scope: the paired run reached 250 steps then died at native level (exit 127) under GPU contention from unrelated workloads — proof the objective is live and correctly wired, not a converged model. Panc1 is a cell line, so this is a PDAC-line residual, not primary tumour; the sealed benchmark is not discharged.

PDAC chromatin — the central gap, now partly closed

The corpus was healthy pancreas only, so the PDAC-minus-healthy residual the whole design rests on was unmeasurable. ENCODE Panc1 now supplies a mark-matched PDAC panel (ATAC 99,761 peaks; H3K27ac 45,824; CTCF 58,083; H3K4me1/me3, H3K27me3, H3K9me3, H3K36me3, plus TCF7L2 — itself a RAC target). First PDAC-chromatin validation of the targets, which were derived without any PDAC chromatin: they are significantly enriched for PDAC accessibility (94% vs 73%, OR 6.2, p=0.029) and PDAC H3K27ac activity (89% vs 63%, OR 4.7, p=0.017).

The disease residual, measured — and replicated on two independent marks. Signal-level on matched assays (ENCODE signal p-value: Panc1 vs healthy pancreas, log2 ratio over TSS±2kb, ~1,676 loci): ATAC — targets +0.279 (70% PDAC-up) vs background −0.263 (43%), p=0.010; H3K27ac — targets +1.596 (80% up) vs background +0.261 (54%), p=0.00062. The ATAC result matters because the overall trend runs the other way (TF promoters are on average less accessible in PDAC) — yet the targets buck it; on H3K27ac the gain is ~3× active-enhancer signal. This is the PDAC-minus-healthy residual the whole design rests on, measured for the first time, and the targets — derived with no chromatin input — are enriched in it on both marks. No sealed study touched.

Three honest limits. (1) Panc1 is a cultured cell line, not a primary tumour — pdac_tumor_bams is still 0 and the registered benchmark asks for tumour/organoid, so this does not discharge it. (2) The peak-overlap differential was an artifact (healthy ATAC 874,795 peaks vs PDAC 99,761, ~9× permissive → "open in PDAC, closed in healthy" empty by construction) — superseded by the signal-level residual above, which is the correct measurement. (3) TCGA has no PAAD ATAC at all — GDC carries ATAC for 23 projects and PAAD is not one; Corces 2018 excluded pancreatic. A hard fact, not a fetch failure.

Protein + 3D layers (new)

CPTAC mass-spec (238 tumors, 11,419 proteins) quantifies 651 TFs at protein level — 32× the RPPA panel. It reinforces the methylation verdict: AGR2 protein is −0.489 and detected in 100% of tumors — reliably measured and low — so methylation and proteomics independently agree AGR2 is a poor CRISPRi target. FOSL1 (15%) and ZNF331 (16%) are barely detectable at protein level and get deprioritised. 4DN PANC-1 Hi-C adds the A/B compartment eigenvector, insulation and TAD boundaries: 90% of RAC targets sit in the active A compartment (vs 70% of background TFs) — a sanity check that passes, though not a discriminator (Mann-Whitney p=0.50; expressed TFs are already mostly in A).

Methylation silencing filter (new)

Real HM450 promoter methylation (183 tumors) now filters the targets. The top hits are open and unmethylated (GATA6, KLF5, MYC, E2F1, SETDB1 all β≈0.02–0.05) — genuinely active, good CRISPRi targets. AGR2 (β=0.586) and FAM83A (β=0.543) are demoted: already silenced, so poor CRISPRi targets despite AGR2's 28% amplification. TP63 (β=0.733) — the basal master TF — is silenced, exactly as expected in a classical-dominant cohort. And CDKN2A is not methylated (β=0.038): it is silenced by deletion (63%) instead — the two mechanisms cleanly separated by real data.

The two textbook PDAC master transcription factors — GATA6 (classical) and KLF5 (lineage-survival, DepMap essential 0.74) — both surface near the top of an unsupervised model that never saw the CRISPR data. Adding real copy-number sharpened the list toward amplified, essential proliferation regulators (E2F1, MYBL2, SETDB1).

Long-range chromatin model

The Enformer-scale comparator (PDACircuitFormer) — built, runs on GPU, honestly not yet a winner.

Trained to convergence (this run)

First real training on all 33,156 healthy shards (196,608 bp): profile loss 0.0088 → 0.0079 at ~step 700, then plateau. Across 8 independent seeds the held-out profile correlation is 0.7102 ± 0.0091 (0.698–0.728) — the model predicts unseen healthy-pancreas chromatin at r ≈ 0.71, reproducibly.

Why it can't beat Enformer yet

the local ChIP corpus is healthy pancreas — 0 PDAC tumor BAMs. The benchmark fails closed until independent PDAC tumor/perturbation chromatin plus hash-locked baselines satisfy every registered axis.

The model now genuinely learns the healthy-pancreas chromatin landscape (the counterfactual prior of the design) — profile correlation 0.73 on held-out windows. But the falsifiable "beats Enformer" headline still requires PDAC tumor/perturbation chromatin and hash-locked baselines on all seven axes. On that benchmark, today: ABSTAIN, by construction — now with a trained prior in hand rather than an untrained shell.

08

Honest limitations

Stated plainly, because the whole project is built to abstain rather than overclaim.

  • Healthy-only chromatin. All 54 ChIP BAMs are healthy/endocrine pancreas; there is zero PDAC-tumor ChIP on disk. Tumor-specific enhancer discovery needs the datasets in the roadmap.
  • RAC essentiality is modest. AUC ≈ 0.67, best-powered at threshold 0.3–0.4; it does not resolve lineage-selective dependency and its coupling does not predict co-essentiality.
  • Composition confound. Bulk co-expression mixes tumor, stroma and immune signal; the first RAC prototype on TCGA failed precisely here, which is why the graph was rebuilt on tumor-intrinsic cell lines.
  • Selective dependency is a power ceiling, not a confound (corrected). Single-cell malignant-only graphs were built and tested; a power audit over 1,164 TFs with DepMap CRISPR finds only 7 PDAC-selective TFs (sel > 0.25). No graph substrate can resolve selective dependency with this readout — an earlier hypothesis blaming the composition confound is refuted by this data.
  • Cross-platform specificity. Tumor-vs-normal uses TCGA RSEM against GTEx TPM — only the sign of the direction is trusted.
  • Convergent targets are hypotheses. They converge multiple real signals but are not experimentally validated dependencies.
  • Provenance note. The TCGA-PAAD expression cache was refreshed from cBioPortal during this session (superset of genes); the manifest hash was re-recorded transparently and loaders now read the cache read-only.
